UXIM 16th, Head of Academics

Yonsei HCI/UX society, designing and running a semester of academic seminarsFall 2026, 12 regular sessions, 4 project teams

Define

Handed-down lecture materials too long, with no way for learners to see which stage they were at

A one-way curriculum, with no path back when research results called for it

Process

12-session curriculum rebuilt around the four Double Diamond stages: Discover (W3–4), Define (W5–6), midterm presentations, Develop and Deliver (W8–11), Demo Day

Seminar materials made and led every week. Each session designed as goals, case, module, quiz, summary, assignment

Outside methods selected for the materials, such as FIRM2NESS, a research-question standard from clinical research, adapted for UX research

Weekly feedback on 4 team projects, midterm feedback sheets

Result

Regular sessions in progress, through Demo Day on December 1

Handover system for the next cohort, with a curriculum template and a weekly revision plan

Tool

Claude Code, PowerPoint, FigJam
