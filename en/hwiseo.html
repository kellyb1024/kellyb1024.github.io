Hwiseo 휘서

Mobile app and motion graphics, an AI friend that asks about the stories in your photos2026, 12th IID Exhibition, Solo

Define

Taking photos got easy, but the time to look back at them disappeared

In an age when generative AI can make anything, the value of moments people actually captured and lived gets buried

The need is not a tool that sorts photos, but a reason to bring forgotten moments back and talk about them

Solution

Hwiseo, an AI friend, shines a light on one forgotten photo and asks the person who lived that moment

AI asks, people tell: no made-up memories, users unfold their own

Rather than storing memories, it stays beside you and keeps the stories as stickers and a scrapbook

Process

Hwiseo designed as a small candle that lights up photos, instead of an AI with a human face

Conversation flow and prompt design for Hwiseo, ruling out leading questions and generated memories

One visual language built around the character, from app screens to booth print

Booth of five art boxes and a four-step stamp tour (video, app, receipt photo, postcard decorating). Receipt photo camera built with Python and OpenCV to run even on the venue's old iMac

Result

At the 12th IID Exhibition "WHY NOT?!", 200+ visitors talked with Hwiseo and took home receipt photos and postcards

Released as a web app anyone can try

Interview in KALEIIDOZINE Issue 8

Artist statement

Generative AI can make almost anything look real, yet in its world, the act of living is erased. In an age of instant generation, what stays valuable about being human is lived experience. Hwiseo, a small candle AI friend, shines a light on a forgotten photo and asks about it, so the person who lived that moment can unfold their story again. AI asks and people tell: a use of AI beyond generation. Rather than storing memories, Hwiseo stays beside you so you can keep living with them.
