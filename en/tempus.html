TEMPUS studio

MD, January to July 2023. Concepts, graphics, lookbooks and storytelling for two seasons, SS and FW

Define

New graphics every season from 2018 to 2023, but no brand story linking the seasons. 2018 and 2020 had no season story at all

Process

Season archive

Images and keywords for every season from 2018 SS to 2023 MID, one page each

Redefining the brand

TEMPUS, Latin for "time", redefined as three words: TIME, MOMENTUM, US. A brand introduction about "me", living fully in the now, and "us", enjoying that time

The one who can express TEMPUS is you.

2023 MID season, "IRIDESCENT MYSELF"

A season story likening the inner self to a kaleidoscope whose colors shift with the light it takes in. Color system, deconstruction into patterns and colors, and season words, all set down as design guidelines

2023 FW season, "City Boy"

A season reinterpreting the basics everyone pulls out each year through layers and volume. Restrained graphics and old-school type for comfort in complicated times. The balance between the city boy look and TEMPUS's street identity proposed in four levels, Basic, Inter, Advan, Mood, and applied to sweatshirts and hoodies

Result

Lookbooks and season storytelling published on the brand website, a story that carries from season to season and strengthens the brand image
