BUROCK

Rebranding proposal for the Busan International Rock Festival. From a one-day festival visit to one more day in Busan

Role
Character design, website planning and build (solo)
Landmark container signage, dialect slogans
Team
4 people
Period
Fall 2026, Visual Systems
Result
Website launched
burock-onemoreday.vercel.app

Define

A face that changes every year, with no Busan in it

Logo and key visual change almost every year, with nothing of Busan's character in them

80,000 come, and leave the same day

81% of the audience came from outside Busan, yet their spending stayed inside Samnak-dong. Target: visitors from outside Busan who head home the same day

Source: Busan Ilbo, October 11, 2026 (Busan city card data)

Process

Mascot band character sheets

Busan dialect slogans

Landmark container booths

Booths made from six used Busan Port containers painted with Busan landmarks. Bought once, reused every year

Visual system

Logo, poster, waste collection zone, merch

Result

The day after the festival, one more day in Busan

Three day courses linking lesser-known neighborhoods, travel times checked on Naver Map for each leg, market and festival schedules. The six container landmarks link to the site

Open burock-onemoreday.vercel.app full size

If they stay one more day

About 1.1 billion won in local spending if 10% of out-of-town visitors stay one more day (team estimate)

Sources: Busan Ilbo, October 11, 2026 and August 18, 2025 / Busan Tourism Organization, 2024 Busan Visitor Survey / BusinessWatch, August 2, 2023
