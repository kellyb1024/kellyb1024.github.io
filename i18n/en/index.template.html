Beyond AI, we all have our own story.

I'm Kyungjin Bae,
a product designer who shapes user experiences.

It starts with a story

A good service doesn't begin on a screen. It begins with hearing the story someone hasn't yet found the words for.

I'm most absorbed when digging into the context behind the numbers, and putting into words a discomfort users can't quite explain, even to themselves.

Then I connect those stories to technology. I believe AI can be a channel that carries the stories of people held back by institutions and language out to the world.

Looking closely at users at the edge widens the options for everyone.

GachiWork

Mobile app, a peer community for migrant workers in Korea2026, Yonsei TAP Capstone, Team lead

Define

340,000 migrant workers on E‑9 visas in factories and farms, tied to assigned workplaces and scattered across the country

Dorm fees come out of their pay, with no coworker to ask what others pay

Hard to tell whether what they face is common practice or illegal

Solution

Questions asked in one's native language, translated and sent to peers from other countries doing the same work

Peer answers and law-based official answers on one screen

When risks like unpaid wages are suspected, an alert to the user and a direct link to an expert inside the app

Result

Grand Prize (Teacher's Pick), Yonsei TAP Capstone

Selected for the Yonsei IHEI workstation

App prototype covering one flow, from question to expert connection

1 invention disclosure, pilot with real workers in development

Role

As team lead, led the work from service planning to UX/UI design and prototype development

Research design, scheduling, partnership prep with the Korea Labor Foundation

Video, presentation deck, poster

Tool

Claude Code, Adobe Illustrator

Hwiseo 휘서

Mobile app and motion graphics, an AI friend that asks about the stories in your photos2026, 12th IID Graduation Exhibition

Define

Taking photos got easy, but the time to look back at them disappeared

In an age when generative AI can make anything, the value of moments people actually captured and lived gets buried

The need is not a tool that sorts photos, but a reason to bring forgotten moments back and talk about them

Solution

Hwiseo, an AI friend, shines a light on one forgotten photo and asks the person who lived that moment

AI asks, people tell: no made-up memories, users unfold their own

Rather than storing memories, it stays beside you and keeps the stories as stickers and a scrapbook

Result

Shown at the 12th IID Graduation Exhibition "WHY NOT?!", where 200+ visitors talked with Hwiseo and took home receipt photos and postcards

Released as a web app anyone can try

Interview in KALEIIDOZINE Issue 8

Role

Built the whole app on my own, from planning to design and development

Head TA of the same exhibition, leading its branding and operations

Character, booth, receipt photo camera, promo video

Tool

Claude Code, Adobe Illustrator

SUNI C

Web dashboard, campaign scheduling for SK Broadband2026, SK mySUNI future skills program for university students, SUNI C 5th cohort, AI team project, Team lead

Define

SK Broadband has to fit campaigns from many marketing teams within each channel's daily send limit

Requests come in through Google Sheets and one coordinator calculates by hand, while the remaining-capacity table shows only confirmed requests

Marketers and the coordinator never see the same status, so checks and adjustments go back and forth without end

Solution

Remaining send capacity always on the calendar by date, so no need to ask the coordinator

Automatic capacity check the moment a request is made, with 3 alternative dates suggested right away if it doesn't fit

Only checked requests that need adjusting go to the coordinator

Result

Top Prize, SUNI C 5th cohort

Dashboard designed to cut the coordinator's steps from request to confirmation from 5 to 1

Plus an adoption kit that takes the team's existing Google Sheets as is

Role

As team lead, led development of the dashboard screens

Led schedules and meetings, taught Claude Code to teammates

Video, presentation

Tool

Claude Code, Adobe Illustrator, Higgsfield
