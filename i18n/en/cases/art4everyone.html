Art4Everyone

Mobile web app, a museum companion guide for ages 50–642026, selected for the Yonsei Institute for Higher Education Innovation (IHEI) workstation, Team lead

Define

People aged 50–64 are assumed to visit museums less because they struggle with digital tools, yet all 31 surveyed used smartphones with ease

The real barriers were fatigue from standing and small label text. Existing museum apps keep eyes on the screen for the whole visit

No way to see less but see well, at one's own stamina and pace

Solution

Enter the time you have, and get only the works that fit it

AR guidance to the next work, and the nearest rest area shown before you tire

Large type and one action per screen, so less time on the phone and more on the art

Process

Team of 4. Visits to three senior centers, survey of 31 people aged 50–64, 4 focus groups (15 people), observation at the National Museum of Korea

Instead of adding features like existing museum apps, chose to keep people off the phone

Refined through remote testing with 6 people

Result

Selected for the IHEI workstation

Web app prototype covering one flow, from entering visit time to rest guidance

Role

As team lead, led the project and built everything from UI design to the web app myself

Ran the research, communicated with senior centers

Gave the final presentation

Tool

Claude Code, Adobe Illustrator
